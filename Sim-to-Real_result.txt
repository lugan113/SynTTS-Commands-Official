3. Key Findings
Bridging the Domain Gap: In the Zero-shot scenario for Chinese Wake Words, the model showed high precision but very low recall (44% for "Hi_Xiaowen"). This indicates that the model learned "synthetic-specific" features.
The Power of Few-shot: By adding only 50 real-world samples (representing less than 1% of the total training data) and applying Aggressive Oversampling, the accuracy for Chinese Wake Words jumped from 67.83% to 95.77%.
Consistency: The improvement was consistent across both Chinese and English datasets, proving that the methodology is language-agnostic and highly robust for real-world KWS deployment.
